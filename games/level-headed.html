The Gumball Shop Scale

Every paper bag holds the same secret number of gumballs, and the shop scale is balanced. Change one pan and the scale tips, so you have to make the same change to the other pan to level it again. Keep going until one bag sits alone. No clock.

Tip: tap a gumball, bag or balloon on the scale to take it off just that pan (tapping a balloon pops it).
